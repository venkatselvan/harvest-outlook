# Agricultural soil reference sample

The active sample is **ICAR paddy-field soil analysis — Vizianagaram (2019)**. The official [NITI Aayog publication](https://www.niti.gov.in/node/678) hosts ICAR-NAARM's July 2020 report, *Adoption of Natural Farming and its Effect on Crop Yield and Farmers' Livelihood in India*. Its context and methods describe actual farmer paddy-field composite soil sampling in April–May 2019, with laboratory analysis at ICAR-CRIDA.

`icar-naarm-agricultural-soil-reference-2020.pdf` preserves original PDF pages **54, 120, 122 and 123**, without altering their page content. These cover sampling context, methods, and plot-wise chemical tables IV(a)–IV(c). The excerpt is 174,075 bytes; SHA-256 is `e753a2bc98ad179d9898daaddb8d8ccce169daaa2cf72a22b2d1db451b5ffea8`. Full source provenance, the original-document hash, page mapping and limitations are in `../reference-soil-report.json`.

In Farm settings → Soil reports, choose **Load public sample**. The reference extraction is explicitly limited to the **GRL row / NF (Natural Farming) column's twelve chemical readings** on retained pages 3–4. The surrounding pages remain intact as evidence. Other village rows, Non-NF plots and the microbial table are not selected and must not be mixed into that sample. This is a selected agricultural research reference, not a complete-table extraction or an individual farmer Soil Health Card.

No pH or electrical conductivity reading is supplied in the selected chemical tables. Exact collection and publication days are not provided; period-only dates must remain unknown in exact-date fields. Available N/P/K and other analyte forms/methods retain the report's wording. No numeric reading is preloaded into the extraction output or any farm facts. The actual configured Meta model parses the pages after an explicit request; all results remain pending human review, and reference samples cannot be confirmed as measurements of the selected farm. Uploading the exact registered PDF manually also isolates it as this reference.
